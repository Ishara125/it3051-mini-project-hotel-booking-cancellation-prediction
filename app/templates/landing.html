Why Cancellation Prediction Matters

Hotel booking cancellations create real operational and financial challenges. Earlier risk information gives management more time to respond.

Revenue Protection

Unexpected cancellations can leave rooms unsold and reduce potential revenue. Earlier risk estimates support more proactive inventory management.

Occupancy Planning

Earlier risk information can help managers make better inventory and overbooking decisions aligned with actual expected demand.

Operational Planning

More accurate occupancy expectations can support staffing, housekeeping and resource planning across departments.

Guest Strategy

High-risk reservations can be handled using appropriate retention, confirmation, or deposit strategies at the right time.

Process

How It Works

From raw booking data to a business-ready cancellation risk estimate — five clear steps.

1

Enter Booking Details

The user provides reservation, guest, stay, and booking-history information across 26 structured input fields covering the full booking context.

2

Validate Information

The system validates input values and rejects invalid or incomplete data before any prediction is attempted, protecting pipeline integrity.

3

Prepare the Booking

The same feature-engineering and preprocessing logic used during model development transforms the booking into 899 numeric features.

4

Predict Cancellation Risk

The trained Tuned Gradient Boosting model evaluates the processed reservation and outputs a calibrated cancellation probability.

5

Display Business-Friendly Result

The system returns a clear outcome — Likely to Cancel or Likely Not to Cancel — along with the cancellation probability and a LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk classification.
